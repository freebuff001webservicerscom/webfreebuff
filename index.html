Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start building.

SCTHSM
Still curious? Talk to a human
How long does it take to build my website?

Most websites go live within 7 to 14 days. After a short kickoff call we produce your first design draft in 48 hours, then refine it together until it reflects your brand perfectly.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Just your logo, any brand assets you already have, and a few photos. Our copywriters can draft the text for you if you don't have it yet.

Do you manage Google and social ads too?

Yes. We set up, launch, and optimize Google Search, Google Shopping, Facebook, and Instagram campaigns. You get one dashboard and a plain-language report each month.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Our plans are month to month, and you can cancel or pause at any time. The website you paid for is yours to keep.

Can you improve my existing website instead?

Absolutely. Many clients start with a speed, SEO, and conversion audit of their current site. We fix what's holding it back before rebuilding anything.
