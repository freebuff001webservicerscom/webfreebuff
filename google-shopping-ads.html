Do I need a Merchant Center account?

You do, but we can create and verify it for you. It stays in your name and we work inside it with your permission.

How many products can you handle?

Our Premium plan has no catalogue limit. We regularly work with feeds of 50,000+ SKUs.

What are CSS savings?

Google allows Comparison Shopping Services to bid on your behalf with a 20% discount on clicks in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. We pass that saving straight through.
