Still curious? Visit the help centre
How fast can my campaigns go live?

Most accounts are live within 48 hours of onboarding. We need access to your ad account, your site and a 30-minute kickoff call.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work inside your Google Ads account, so every campaign, keyword and conversion stays yours if we ever part ways.

Is the ad budget included in the plan?

No — the plan covers management and creative. Ad spend goes directly to Google and you can set any monthly ceiling you like.
