How much ad spend do I need?

We recommend at least $600/month in spend to gather enough data in the first month. There is no maximum.

Do you create the images and video?

Yes. Creative production is included in Standard and Premium, and available as an add-on on Basic.

Can you work with my existing pixel?

Absolutely. We audit the existing setup first and fix what is missing before launching anything.
