Still curious? Visit the help centre
Do you need access to my Google account?

We ask you to add us as a manager on the Business Profile. You keep ownership and can remove access at any time.

Can you remove bad reviews?

Only Google can remove a review, and only when it breaks policy. We handle the report and help you respond well to the rest.

How long does verification take?

Video verification usually completes in 3–5 working days. We prepare the evidence and walk you through the recording.
